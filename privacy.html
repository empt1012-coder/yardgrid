Privacy

Without an account, Bloom & Bed Garden keeps your garden on this device. Beds, plant lists, and your frost date are saved in the browser. One garden plan and one arrangement stay there. Export writes a JSON file you keep.

A yard photo stays in the browser too, in IndexedDB, unless you save it to a Plus account. Large photos are shrunk to a long edge of 1,600 pixels and saved as JPEG. Pictures you add in Placement mode are shrunk as well. A PNG can keep a transparent background. The built-in plant library is part of this site. Those pictures are generated. They are not your uploads.

Bloom & Bed Plus stores the gardens you choose to save, the email you sign in with, and the pictures that belong to those gardens. Pictures are shrunk before they are stored. Each account has a 200 MB cap. A view-only share link can be opened by anyone who has it. It does not let them edit the saved copy. If Plus ends, the saved gardens stay and can be opened and exported. New cloud saves stop until Plus is on again. They are not deleted because a subscription ended.

Payments are handled by Stripe. This site does not store your card number. Stripe receives the email and the subscription details needed to charge and to cancel.

To delete the account and the gardens stored in it, sign in on the planner and use Delete account. That removes the cloud copies and the sign-in. A file you already exported stays on your device. The browser copy on that device stays until you clear the site’s data in the browser.

If you export a garden file, that file contains the plan and, when you had a photo, the picture itself. Treat the file like any other photo you save.

The frost-date help link opens an outside site in a new tab. That visit is between you and that site.

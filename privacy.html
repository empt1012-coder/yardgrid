Privacy

Bloom & Bed Garden keeps your garden on this device. Beds, plant lists, and your frost date are saved in the browser’s local storage. There is no account and no server.

A yard photo stays in the browser too. It is stored in IndexedDB on this device, and it is included if you export a JSON file. Large photos are shrunk to a long edge of 1,600 pixels and saved as JPEG before they are stored, so they take less space. The photo is not uploaded.

Pictures you add in Placement mode stay on this device as well. They are shrunk and stored in IndexedDB, and an export includes them. They are not uploaded. A PNG can keep a transparent background. A JPG keeps the background that was in the file. The built-in plant library is part of this site. Those pictures are generated for the site. They are not your uploads.

If you export a garden file, that file contains the plan and, when you had a photo, the picture itself. Treat the file like any other photo you save.

The frost-date help link opens an outside site in a new tab. That visit is between you and that site.

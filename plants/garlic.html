Garlic: spacing, plants per square foot, and when to plant

Garlic: 9 per square foot. Days to harvest: 180–210 days. Plant cloves 1–2 weeks after the average first frost.

Per square foot9 per square foot
Squares used1
Spacing9 per square at 4 in (SDSU). UF/IFAS lists 4 per square. This grid follows SDSU because that sheet is the square-foot count we use when it names the crop.
Days to harvest180–210 days. Days to harvest from the UF/IFAS Leon County square-foot guide. That guide is for Northwest Florida.
Plant cloves1–2 weeks after the average first frost. UMN: plant cloves in the fall, usually one or two weeks after the first killing frost. The dates here count from the average first 32°F date. UF/IFAS uses October–November in Northwest Florida, and that month range is not turned into a frost offset.
Timing noteUF/IFAS plants garlic in October–November in Northwest Florida. That month range is not your frost date. Check a local extension note before you plant.
LightFull sun is the usual bed for this list. A hours figure for garlic is not in the sources used here.
How to plantPlant cloves, not seed (UF/IFAS).

Garlic: spacing, plants per square foot, and when to plant

Garlic: 9 per square foot. Days to harvest: 180–210 days. UF/IFAS plants garlic in October–November in Northwest Florida. That month range is not your frost date. Check a local extension note before you plant.

LightFull sun is the usual bed for this list. A hours figure for garlic is not in the sources used here.
How to plantPlant cloves, not seed (UF/IFAS).

No frost-offset dates are shown for garlic, because the sources used here do not give one.

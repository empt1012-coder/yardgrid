Kale: spacing, plants per square foot, and when to plant

Kale: 1 per square foot. Days to harvest: 50–55 days. Direct sow 4 weeks before the average last frost.

Per square foot1 per square foot
Squares used1
Spacing1 per square (UF/IFAS). SDSU’s count list does not name kale.
Days to harvest50–55 days. SDSU sample layout table. UF/IFAS lists 70–80 for Northwest Florida. Both are real published ranges.
Direct sow4 weeks before the average last frost. CSU’s mountain-county guide: direct-seed frost-tolerant plants, including kale, four weeks before the last frost.
Timing noteSDSU also lists transplanting kale, but that sheet does not give a week count.
LightUMN lists baby kale among crops that can grow with less than a few hours of sun.
How to plantDirect sow or transplant (SDSU, UF/IFAS).

Cucumber: spacing, plants per square foot, and when to plant

Cucumber: 1 per square foot. Days to harvest: 55 days. Direct sow around the average last frost.

Per square foot1 per square foot
Squares used1
Spacing1 per square (UF/IFAS). CSU says 6 in if trellised (about 4 per square) and 24–36 in if not. This grid uses the roomier UF count. Trellis it if you only give it one square.
Days to harvest55 days. Typical days to harvest. CSU GardenNotes #720. UF/IFAS lists 50–65. SDSU lists 50–70.
Direct sowaround the average last frost. Tender: sow around the average last spring frost (CSU).
Timing noteIf you transplant, wait until frost risk has passed. CSU says vine-crop seedlings should be only 2–3 weeks old, and that direct sowing does better.
LightFull sun is the usual bed for this summer crop.
How to plantDirect sow preferred (CSU).

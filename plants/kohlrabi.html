Kohlrabi: spacing, plants per square foot, and when to plant

Kohlrabi: 1 per square foot. Days to harvest: 50 days. Direct sow 2–4 weeks before the average last frost.

Per square foot1 per square foot
Squares used1
Spacing1 per square. CSU’s block spacing is 7–9 in, so four plants would be tighter than that sheet. UF/IFAS lists 4 per square. This grid stays with the wider CSU figure.
Days to harvest50 days. Typical days to harvest. CSU GardenNotes #720. UF/IFAS lists 70–80.
Direct sow2–4 weeks before the average last frost. Hardy: as early as 2–4 weeks before the average last spring frost (CSU).
LightFull sun is the usual bed. A separate hours figure is not in the sources used here.
How to plantDirect sow. CSU does not list a transplant age for kohlrabi.

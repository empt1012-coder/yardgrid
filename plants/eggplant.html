Eggplant: spacing, plants per square foot, and when to plant

Eggplant: 1 per square foot. Days to harvest: 60 days from transplant. Start indoors 4–7 weeks before the average last frost; set out 2 weeks after the average last frost.

Per square foot1 per square foot
Squares used1
Spacing1 per square (SDSU and UF/IFAS). CSU’s block spacing is 18–24 in. This grid follows the square-foot sheets. Use more squares if you follow CSU.
Days to harvest60 days from transplant. CSU GardenNotes #720, from transplant. UF/IFAS lists 90–110, which may be counted differently. Both figures are shown so you can see the gap.
Start indoors4–7 weeks before the average last frost. CSU: transplants are 6–9 weeks old. Earliest set-out used here is 2 weeks after the last frost.
Set out2 weeks after the average last frost. Earliest set-out, 2 or more weeks after the average last spring frost (CSU very tender crops).
LightFull sun, at least 6–8 hours (UMN raised beds, for eggplants).

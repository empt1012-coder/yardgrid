Watermelon: spacing, plants per square foot, and when to plant

Watermelon: 1 plant per 2 squares. Days to harvest: 85 days. Start indoors 0–1 week before the average last frost; set out 2 weeks after the average last frost; direct sow 2 weeks after the average last frost.

Per square foot1 plant per 2 squares
Squares used2
Spacing1 plant on 2 squares if trellised. UF/IFAS uses watermelon as the example of 24 in vining spacing, which is 1 per 2 squares with a trellis. On the ground, CSU says 36–48 in.
Days to harvest85 days. Typical days to harvest. CSU GardenNotes #720.
Start indoors0–1 week before the average last frost. Only if you transplant. Seedlings 2–3 weeks old (CSU).
Set out2 weeks after the average last frost. Earliest set-out if you use a transplant (CSU).
Direct sow2 weeks after the average last frost. Very tender: typically 2 or more weeks after the last frost (CSU). Direct sowing is preferred.
LightFull sun is the usual bed for this summer vine.
How to plantDirect sow preferred, trellised in this grid.

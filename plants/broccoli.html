Broccoli: spacing, plants per square foot, and when to plant

Broccoli: 1 per square foot. Days to harvest: 65 days from transplant. Start indoors 7–11 weeks before the average last frost; set out 2–4 weeks before the average last frost.

Per square foot1 per square foot
Squares used1
Spacing1 per square (SDSU, 12 in). UF/IFAS says 1 per 2 square feet. CSU’s block spacing is 18 in. This grid follows SDSU. Give it more room if plants crowd.
Days to harvest65 days from transplant. CSU GardenNotes #720. Days are from transplant, not from seed. UF/IFAS lists 75–90. SDSU’s sample table lists cabbage separately; broccoli days are not in that table.
Start indoors7–11 weeks before the average last frost. CSU: transplants are 5–7 weeks old, and hardy crops can go out as early as 2–4 weeks before the last frost. The date span is that wide on purpose.
Set out2–4 weeks before the average last frost. As early as 2–4 weeks before the average last spring frost (CSU hardy crops).
LightFull sun is the usual bed. A separate hours figure for broccoli is not in the sources used here.
How to plantUsually transplanted in spring (CSU).

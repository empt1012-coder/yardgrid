Leek: spacing, plants per square foot, and when to plant

Leek: 4 per square foot. Days to harvest: 120 days. Start indoors 6–8 weeks before the average last frost; set out 2 weeks before the average last frost.

Per square foot4 per square foot
Squares used1
Spacing4 per square, from CSU’s wider block spacing of 6 in (the listed range is 4–6 in). Closer spacing is only for improved soil.
Days to harvest120 days. Typical days to harvest. CSU GardenNotes #720.
Start indoors6–8 weeks before the average last frost. CSU mountain-county guide: sow leeks indoors 6–8 weeks before the last frost.
Set out2 weeks before the average last frost. That same guide says to plant them out two weeks before the last frost.
LightFull sun is the usual bed. A separate hours figure is not in the sources used here.
How to plantTransplant. GardenNotes #720 does not list a transplant age; the weeks come from the mountain guide.

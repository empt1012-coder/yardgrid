Spinach: spacing, plants per square foot, and when to plant

Spinach: 9 per square foot. Days to harvest: 40 days. Direct sow 2–4 weeks before the average last frost.

Per square foot9 per square foot
Squares used1
Spacing9 per square at 4 in (SDSU). CSU’s wider block figure is 4–6 in.
Days to harvest40 days. Typical days to harvest. CSU GardenNotes #720.
Direct sow2–4 weeks before the average last frost. Hardy: as early as 2–4 weeks before the average last spring frost (CSU).
LightCool-season green. UMN does not list spinach with the low-light crops; give it a bright bed.
How to plantDirect sow.

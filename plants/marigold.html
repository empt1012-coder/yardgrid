Marigold: spacing, plants per square foot, and when to plant

Marigold: 4 per square foot. Days to harvest: 2 months as a cover. Start indoors 4 weeks before the average last frost; direct sow 2 weeks before the average last frost.

Per square foot4 per square foot
Squares used1
Spacing4 per square is 6 in apart. Clemson says a nematode planting must be solid, no more than 7 in apart. This is inside that spacing, not a bloom-spacing claim.
Days to harvest2 months as a cover. Not a vegetable harvest. UF/IFAS ENY-056: for nematode suppression, grow marigolds at least two months on the spot where the next crop will go.
Start indoors4 weeks before the average last frost. The same CSU table lists 4 weeks indoors.
Direct sow2 weeks before the average last frost. CSU’s annuals table marks marigold as a flower best direct seeded about two weeks before the average frost-free date.
LightA flower bed in sun. A hours figure is not in the sources used here.
How to plantDirect sow or start indoors (CSU). For nematodes, a solid block matters more than a flower dotted in the bed (Clemson, LSU).

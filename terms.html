Terms

Bloom & Bed Garden is a personal garden planner. The planning tools, charts, Save image, and Print are free.

Bloom & Bed Plus is an optional subscription at $5 a month or $30 a year. It is for your own gardens, not for resale or for running someone else’s business on the account. You can cancel anytime. Cancellation stops the next charge. It does not delete gardens already saved. Those stay available to open and export.

Payments are taken by Stripe. The price does not include tax Stripe may add.

Planting dates are averages from the sources named on the site. They are not a promise about your yard, your frost, or a harvest.

These terms were last updated on 21 November 2026.

About

Bloom & Bed Garden is a simple planner for garden beds and for arranging flowers and plants. You draw beds in feet, drop common vegetables onto 1-foot squares, and read planting dates from your average last spring frost. In Placement mode you arrange plants and flowers on a photo of your space, using the built-in pictures or your own.

Spacing, days to harvest, and dates come from public extension guides. The sources are listed in SOURCES.md in this folder. Where two guides disagree, the plant card says so. This is not a substitute for your local extension office or the seed packet.

The photo of your yard stays in your browser. There is no account.

This is a working prototype. The “Buy seeds” links are placeholders. Nothing is for sale on this copy.

About

Bloom & Bed Garden is a simple planner for garden beds and for arranging flowers and plants. You draw beds in feet, drop common vegetables onto 1-foot squares, and read planting dates from your average last spring frost. In Placement mode you arrange plants and flowers on a photo of your space, using the built-in pictures or your own.

Spacing, days to harvest, and dates come from public extension guides. The sources are listed in SOURCES.md. Where two guides disagree, the plant card says so. This is not a substitute for your local extension office or the seed packet.

The planner, the charts, Save image, and Print are free, with no account. Your plan and the photo of your yard stay in your browser.

Bloom & Bed Plus is optional. It adds an account for saving more gardens, opening them on another device, and view-only share links.

Questions or a date that looks wrong: contact.

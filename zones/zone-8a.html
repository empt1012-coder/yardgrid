Zone 8a planting calendar and frost dates

Zone 8a median last frost: Mar 30. Median first frost: Nov 6.

In zone 8a, the median last 32°F date across 564 NOAA stations is Mar 30, and the median first 32°F date is Nov 6. That leaves about 221 days between the two medians. Half of those stations have their last frost between Mar 25 and Apr 4, and their first frost between Nov 2 and Nov 10. The states with the most of them are Texas, North Carolina, Arkansas, and Alabama. Counting from the median, the earliest sowings here (radish, green onion, spinach) start around Mar 2, and tomatoes go out from Apr 13.

A zone is a winter-low band from the USDA map, not a frost date. These medians are calculated here from NOAA station normals for stations matched to zone 8a in the contiguous United States. NOAA does not publish one frost date per zone. 2596 of 3421 zone 8a ZIP codes (76%) have a NOAA station with both 32°F dates within 80 km. Your own ZIP gives a closer date:

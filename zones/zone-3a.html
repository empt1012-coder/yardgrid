Zone 3a planting calendar and frost dates

Zone 3a median last frost: May 21. Median first frost: Sep 22.

In zone 3a, the median last 32°F date across 2 NOAA stations is May 21, and the median first 32°F date is Sep 22. That leaves about 124 days between the two medians. Those stations have their last frost between May 12 and May 29, and their first frost between Sep 13 and Oct 1. All of them are in Minnesota. That is a small sample, so treat the zone median as rough and use your ZIP. Counting from the median, the earliest sowings here (radish, green onion, spinach) start around Apr 23, and tomatoes go out from Jun 4.

A zone is a winter-low band from the USDA map, not a frost date. These medians are calculated here from NOAA station normals for stations matched to zone 3a in the contiguous United States. NOAA does not publish one frost date per zone. 5 of 5 zone 3a ZIP codes (100%) have a NOAA station with both 32°F dates within 80 km. Your own ZIP gives a closer date:

Zone 9b planting calendar and frost dates

Zone 9b median last frost: Feb 4. Median first frost: Dec 12.

In zone 9b, the median last 32°F date across 289 NOAA stations is Feb 4, and the median first 32°F date is Dec 12. That leaves about 311 days between the two medians. Half of those stations have their last frost between Jan 28 and Feb 18, and their first frost between Dec 4 and Dec 23. The states with the most of them are California, Texas, Florida, and Arizona. Counting from the median, the earliest sowings here (radish, green onion, spinach) start around Jan 7, and tomatoes go out from Feb 18.

A zone is a winter-low band from the USDA map, not a frost date. These medians are calculated here from NOAA station normals for stations matched to zone 9b in the contiguous United States. NOAA does not publish one frost date per zone. 1474 of 2085 zone 9b ZIP codes (71%) have a NOAA station with both 32°F dates within 80 km. Your own ZIP gives a closer date:

When to plant in zone 9b

Dates count from the Feb 4 median last frost, using the extension windows on each plant page. A range means the guide gave a range. Here the median last and first frosts are only weeks apart in winter, so counting from a spring date is a rough guide. The Florida guide in our sources uses calendar months instead, and those are not converted to dates here.

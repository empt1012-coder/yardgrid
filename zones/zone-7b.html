Zone 7b planting calendar and frost dates

Zone 7b median last frost: Apr 7. Median first frost: Oct 31.

In zone 7b, the median last 32°F date across 517 NOAA stations is Apr 7, and the median first 32°F date is Oct 31. That leaves about 207 days between the two medians. Half of those stations have their last frost between Apr 3 and Apr 13, and their first frost between Oct 26 and Nov 4. The states with the most of them are Tennessee, Oklahoma, Virginia, and North Carolina. Counting from the median, the earliest sowings here (radish, green onion, spinach) start around Mar 10, and tomatoes go out from Apr 21.

A zone is a winter-low band from the USDA map, not a frost date. These medians are calculated here from NOAA station normals for stations matched to zone 7b in the contiguous United States. NOAA does not publish one frost date per zone. 3034 of 3840 zone 7b ZIP codes (79%) have a NOAA station with both 32°F dates within 80 km. Your own ZIP gives a closer date:

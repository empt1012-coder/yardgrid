Zone 1b planting calendar and frost dates

Zone 1b median last frost: May 26. Median first frost: Aug 28.

In zone 1b, the median last 32°F date across 4 NOAA stations is May 26, and the median first 32°F date is Aug 28. That leaves about 94 days between the two medians. Those stations have their last frost between May 18 and Jun 3, and their first frost between Aug 24 and Sep 8. All of them are in Alaska. That is a small sample, so treat the zone median as rough and use your ZIP. Counting from the median, the earliest sowings here (radish, green onion, spinach) start around Apr 28, and tomatoes go out from Jun 9.

A zone is a winter-low band from the USDA map, not a frost date. These medians are calculated here from NOAA station normals for stations matched to zone 1b in Alaska. NOAA does not publish one frost date per zone. 5 of 11 zone 1b ZIP codes (45%) have a NOAA station with both 32°F dates within 80 km. Your own ZIP gives a closer date:

Zone 9a planting calendar and frost dates

Zone 9a median last frost: Mar 4. Median first frost: Nov 25.

In zone 9a, the median last 32°F date across 390 NOAA stations is Mar 4, and the median first 32°F date is Nov 25. That leaves about 266 days between the two medians. Half of those stations have their last frost between Feb 25 and Mar 13, and their first frost between Nov 19 and Dec 1. The states with the most of them are Texas, California, Louisiana, and Florida. Counting from the median, the earliest sowings here (radish, green onion, spinach) start around Feb 4, and tomatoes go out from Mar 18.

A zone is a winter-low band from the USDA map, not a frost date. These medians are calculated here from NOAA station normals for stations matched to zone 9a in the contiguous United States. NOAA does not publish one frost date per zone. 1767 of 2199 zone 9a ZIP codes (80%) have a NOAA station with both 32°F dates within 80 km. Your own ZIP gives a closer date:

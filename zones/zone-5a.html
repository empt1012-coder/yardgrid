Zone 5a planting calendar and frost dates

Zone 5a median last frost: May 10. Median first frost: Oct 2.

In zone 5a, the median last 32°F date across 617 NOAA stations is May 10, and the median first 32°F date is Oct 2. That leaves about 145 days between the two medians. Half of those stations have their last frost between May 1 and May 20, and their first frost between Sep 26 and Oct 7. The states with the most of them are Iowa, Wyoming, South Dakota, and Nebraska. Counting from the median, the earliest sowings here (radish, green onion, spinach) start around Apr 12, and tomatoes go out from May 24.

A zone is a winter-low band from the USDA map, not a frost date. These medians are calculated here from NOAA station normals for stations matched to zone 5a in the contiguous United States. NOAA does not publish one frost date per zone. 2257 of 2393 zone 5a ZIP codes (94%) have a NOAA station with both 32°F dates within 80 km. Your own ZIP gives a closer date:

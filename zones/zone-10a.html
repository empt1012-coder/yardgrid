Zone 10a planting calendar and frost dates

Zone 10a median last frost: Jan 16. Median first frost: Jan 1 (the next winter).

In zone 10a, the median last 32°F date across 142 NOAA stations is Jan 16, and the median first 32°F date is Jan 1 (the next winter). That leaves about 350 days between the two medians. Half of those stations have their last frost between Jan 6 and Jan 25, and their first frost between Dec 25 and Jan 9 (the next winter). The states with the most of them are California, Florida, Texas, and Arizona. Counting from the median, the earliest sowings here (radish, green onion, spinach) start around Dec 19, and tomatoes go out from Jan 30.

A zone is a winter-low band from the USDA map, not a frost date. These medians are calculated here from NOAA station normals for stations matched to zone 10a in the contiguous United States. NOAA does not publish one frost date per zone. 792 of 1234 zone 10a ZIP codes (64%) have a NOAA station with both 32°F dates within 80 km. Your own ZIP gives a closer date:

When to plant in zone 10a

Dates count from the Jan 16 median last frost, using the extension windows on each plant page. A range means the guide gave a range. Here the median last and first frosts are only weeks apart in winter, so counting from a spring date is a rough guide. The Florida guide in our sources uses calendar months instead, and those are not converted to dates here.

Zone 3b planting calendar and frost dates

Zone 3b median last frost: May 18. Median first frost: Sep 23.

In zone 3b, the median last 32°F date across 71 NOAA stations is May 18, and the median first 32°F date is Sep 23. That leaves about 128 days between the two medians. Half of those stations have their last frost between May 15 and May 24, and their first frost between Sep 18 and Sep 26. The states with the most of them are Minnesota, North Dakota, Montana, and Maine. Counting from the median, the earliest sowings here (radish, green onion, spinach) start around Apr 20, and tomatoes go out from Jun 1.

A zone is a winter-low band from the USDA map, not a frost date. These medians are calculated here from NOAA station normals for stations matched to zone 3b in the contiguous United States. NOAA does not publish one frost date per zone. 196 of 204 zone 3b ZIP codes (96%) have a NOAA station with both 32°F dates within 80 km. Your own ZIP gives a closer date:

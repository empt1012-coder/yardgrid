Zone 8b planting calendar and frost dates

Zone 8b median last frost: Mar 19. Median first frost: Nov 12.

In zone 8b, the median last 32°F date across 583 NOAA stations is Mar 19, and the median first 32°F date is Nov 12. That leaves about 238 days between the two medians. Half of those stations have their last frost between Mar 13 and Mar 29, and their first frost between Nov 7 and Nov 17. The states with the most of them are Texas, Oregon, Mississippi, and Alabama. Counting from the median, the earliest sowings here (radish, green onion, spinach) start around Feb 19, and tomatoes go out from Apr 2.

A zone is a winter-low band from the USDA map, not a frost date. These medians are calculated here from NOAA station normals for stations matched to zone 8b in the contiguous United States. NOAA does not publish one frost date per zone. 2648 of 3375 zone 8b ZIP codes (78%) have a NOAA station with both 32°F dates within 80 km. Your own ZIP gives a closer date:

Zone 4b planting calendar and frost dates

Zone 4b median last frost: May 12. Median first frost: Sep 29.

In zone 4b, the median last 32°F date across 395 NOAA stations is May 12, and the median first 32°F date is Sep 29. That leaves about 140 days between the two medians. Half of those stations have their last frost between May 6 and May 21, and their first frost between Sep 23 and Oct 3. The states with the most of them are Montana, South Dakota, Minnesota, and Wisconsin. Counting from the median, the earliest sowings here (radish, green onion, spinach) start around Apr 14, and tomatoes go out from May 26.

A zone is a winter-low band from the USDA map, not a frost date. These medians are calculated here from NOAA station normals for stations matched to zone 4b in the contiguous United States. NOAA does not publish one frost date per zone. 1178 of 1256 zone 4b ZIP codes (94%) have a NOAA station with both 32°F dates within 80 km. Your own ZIP gives a closer date:

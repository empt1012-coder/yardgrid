Zone 5b planting calendar and frost dates

Zone 5b median last frost: May 5. Median first frost: Oct 7.

In zone 5b, the median last 32°F date across 592 NOAA stations is May 5, and the median first 32°F date is Oct 7. That leaves about 155 days between the two medians. Half of those stations have their last frost between Apr 28 and May 15, and their first frost between Oct 1 and Oct 13. The states with the most of them are Nebraska, Colorado, Illinois, and Wisconsin. Counting from the median, the earliest sowings here (radish, green onion, spinach) start around Apr 7, and tomatoes go out from May 19.

A zone is a winter-low band from the USDA map, not a frost date. These medians are calculated here from NOAA station normals for stations matched to zone 5b in the contiguous United States. NOAA does not publish one frost date per zone. 2580 of 2865 zone 5b ZIP codes (90%) have a NOAA station with both 32°F dates within 80 km. Your own ZIP gives a closer date:

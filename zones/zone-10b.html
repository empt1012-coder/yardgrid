Zone 10b planting calendar and frost dates

Zone 10b median last frost: Jan 18. Median first frost: Jan 15 (the next winter).

In zone 10b, the median last 32°F date across 21 NOAA stations is Jan 18, and the median first 32°F date is Jan 15 (the next winter). That leaves about 362 days between the two medians. Half of those stations have their last frost between Jan 13 and Jan 23, and their first frost between Jan 6 (the next winter) and Jan 16 (the next winter). The states with the most of them are Florida and California. Counting from the median, the earliest sowings here (radish, green onion, spinach) start around Dec 21, and tomatoes go out from Feb 1.

A zone is a winter-low band from the USDA map, not a frost date. These medians are calculated here from NOAA station normals for stations matched to zone 10b in the contiguous United States. NOAA does not publish one frost date per zone. 472 of 751 zone 10b ZIP codes (63%) have a NOAA station with both 32°F dates within 80 km. Your own ZIP gives a closer date:

When to plant in zone 10b

Dates count from the Jan 18 median last frost, using the extension windows on each plant page. A range means the guide gave a range. Here the median last and first frosts are only weeks apart in winter, so counting from a spring date is a rough guide. The Florida guide in our sources uses calendar months instead, and those are not converted to dates here.

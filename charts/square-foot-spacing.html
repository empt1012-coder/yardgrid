Square-foot spacing chart

How many plants fit in a 1-foot square, for the 30 plants in the planner. The counts come from the extension sources named on each plant page.

Carrot1616 per square foot16 per square at 3 in. SDSU P-00379 and UF/IFAS. CSU lists 2–3 in.
Radish1616 per square foot16 per square at 3 in. SDSU P-00379 and UF/IFAS. CSU lists 2–3 in.
Green onion1616 per square foot16 per square. SDSU lists green onions at 3 in. UF/IFAS bunching onions are 16. CSU lists 2–3 in.
Beet1616 per square footThis grid uses SDSU’s square-foot count: 16 at 3 in. CSU’s block spacing is wider, 4–6 in (about 4 per square at 6 in), and only the closer figure is suggested for rich soil.
Bush bean99 per square foot9 per square at 4 in (SDSU and UF/IFAS bush beans). CSU’s wider block figure is 6 in, about 4 per square.
Spinach99 per square foot9 per square at 4 in (SDSU). CSU’s wider block figure is 4–6 in.
Garlic99 per square foot9 per square at 4 in (SDSU). UF/IFAS lists 4 per square. This grid follows SDSU because that sheet is the square-foot count we use when it names the crop.
Leaf lettuce44 per square foot4 per square at 6 in (SDSU and UF/IFAS leaf lettuce). CSU’s block spacing for leaf lettuce is 7–9 in, which is wider.
Swiss chard44 per square foot4 per square at 6 in (SDSU). CSU’s block spacing is 7–9 in.
Basil44 per square foot4 per square at 6 in (SDSU). UF/IFAS lists 1 per square. This grid follows SDSU.
Broccoli11 per square foot1 per square (SDSU, 12 in). UF/IFAS says 1 per 2 square feet. CSU’s block spacing is 18 in. This grid follows SDSU. Give it more room if plants crowd.
Cabbage11 per square foot1 per square (SDSU, 12 in). CSU’s block spacing is 18 in, which needs more than one square. This grid follows SDSU.
Pepper11 per square foot1 per square (SDSU and UF/IFAS). CSU’s block spacing is 15–18 in, a bit wider than one foot.
Eggplant11 per square foot1 per square (SDSU and UF/IFAS). CSU’s block spacing is 18–24 in. This grid follows the square-foot sheets. Use more squares if you follow CSU.
Tomato1 / 2 squares1 plant per 2 squares1 plant on 2 squares, the UF/IFAS figure for a staked or caged tomato. CSU lists 24 in for a trellised tomato, which is about 4 square feet. SDSU does not put tomato in its 1-per-square line.
Cucumber11 per square foot1 per square (UF/IFAS). CSU says 6 in if trellised (about 4 per square) and 24–36 in if not. This grid uses the roomier UF count. Trellis it if you only give it one square.
Summer squash1 / 4 squares1 plant per 4 squares1 plant on 4 squares (UF/IFAS, and the 24 in non-vining example in that guide). CSU’s block spacing is wider: 36–48 in.
Pea88 per square foot8 per square along a trellis (UF/IFAS English peas). CSU’s block spacing is 4–6 in, about 4 per square at 6 in. SDSU says a trellis can go at the end of a bed for vining crops.
Kale11 per square foot1 per square (UF/IFAS). SDSU’s count list does not name kale.
Potato11 per square foot1 per square. UF/IFAS says 1. CSU’s block spacing is 12–15 in.
Bulbing onion44 per square foot4 per square. UF/IFAS says 4. CSU’s wider block figure is 6 in, which is also 4 per square. SDSU’s 9-per-square line is green onions and garlic, not bulbing onions.
Leek44 per square foot4 per square, from CSU’s wider block spacing of 6 in (the listed range is 4–6 in). Closer spacing is only for improved soil.
Turnip44 per square foot4 per square, from CSU’s wider 6 in spacing (range 4–6 in). UF/IFAS lists 9 per square. This grid uses the wider CSU figure.
Kohlrabi11 per square foot1 per square. CSU’s block spacing is 7–9 in, so four plants would be tighter than that sheet. UF/IFAS lists 4 per square. This grid stays with the wider CSU figure.
Mustard44 per square foot4 per square (UF/IFAS).
Parsnip44 per square foot4 per square, from CSU’s wider 6 in spacing (range 5–6 in).
Winter squash1 / 2 squares1 plant per 2 squares1 plant on 2 squares if you trellis it (UF/IFAS). On the ground, CSU says 36–48 in. The grid assumes a trellis.
Cantaloupe1 / 2 squares1 plant per 2 squares1 plant on 2 squares if trellised (UF/IFAS melons). On the ground, CSU says 36–48 in. The grid assumes a trellis.
Watermelon1 / 2 squares1 plant per 2 squares1 plant on 2 squares if trellised. UF/IFAS uses watermelon as the example of 24 in vining spacing, which is 1 per 2 squares with a trellis. On the ground, CSU says 36–48 in.
Marigold44 per square foot4 per square is 6 in apart. Clemson says a nematode planting must be solid, no more than 7 in apart. This is inside that spacing, not a bloom-spacing claim.

A plant that uses more than one square is listed as one plant over those squares, not as a fraction of a plant. The spacing guide explains how a 1-foot square is used. To lay the same counts on a bed, open the planner. If you are filling a new box, the raised bed soil calculator gives the soil volume.

Sources: South Dakota State University Extension, “Square-Foot Garden Sample Planting Layout,” P-00379, March 2026, when that sheet lists the crop; otherwise the lower density from UF/IFAS Leon County Extension, “Vegetable Square Foot Planting Guide for Northwest Florida,” or Colorado State University Extension, GardenNotes #720. Conflicts are named in the notes and on each plant page. See SOURCES.md.

Pick a USDA hardiness zone, or type a ZIP code. The bars are extension-guide windows counted from an average last 32°F date.

Dates are averages. Local weather varies, and soil temperature is a better planting signal when you have it. A zone is a winter-low band, not a frost date. ZIP frost dates use the nearest NOAA station within 80 km. A zone with no ZIP uses the median of contiguous-US stations matched to that zone.

Each zone also has its own page with frost dates and a planting table: planting calendars by zone. Spacing and timing for each plant: plant pages.

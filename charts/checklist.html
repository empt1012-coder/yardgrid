Seasonal checklist

Planting windows for this frost date

  • Hardy crops (peas, spinach, lettuce, radishes): as early as 2 to 4 weeks before the last spring frost.
  • Semi-hardy crops (beets, carrots, chard): as early as 0 to 2 weeks before.
  • Tender crops (beans, cucumbers, summer squash): sow around the frost date.
  • Very tender crops (tomatoes, peppers, eggplant): typically 2 or more weeks after.

Those groups are from Colorado State University Extension GardenNotes #720. The full crop-by-crop chart is the planting calendar. For this week’s list, see what to plant this week.

Upper Midwest tasks

From University of Minnesota Extension, “Upper Midwest home garden care schedule.” These months are for that region. They are not rewritten for other zones.

  • March–April: cut back perennials. Mid-March to early May: start summer vegetable seeds indoors.
  • April: cool-season vegetables. Last week of April: carrots, beets, potatoes.
  • Mid to late May or the first week of June: sweet corn, beans, tomatoes, peppers.
  • Last week of May through June: summer vegetables, annuals, and summer bulbs.
  • Mid-July to mid-August: fall vegetables. Late July to early August: divide bearded iris.
  • Late August–September: divide peonies. September to mid-November: plant spring bulbs.
  • October–November: clean up the vegetable garden.

Averages, not a forecast. Soil temperature still matters. UMN calendar. CSU GardenNotes #720.

Zones 3–5

The University of Minnesota Extension Upper Midwest home garden care calendar does not list tasks for December. On that calendar the year starts again in early spring, as soon as the ground thaws. November is the last month it names, for cutting back perennials and finishing vegetable-garden cleanup.

For dates in your own ZIP, use the frost dates tool or what to plant this week.

Zones 6–7

Penn State Extension’s Dauphin County seasonal garden tips are written for USDA zone 6. That series has no December page; January is the next month on the list, and it starts with seed inventory and planning.

For a ZIP-specific frost date, use the frost dates tool.

Zones 8–10

These planting lists are from UF/IFAS for Florida, not for every garden in zones 8–10. North Florida (north of State Road 40) is the closer fit for many zone 8–9 gardens. Central Florida (between State Roads 40 and 70) is the closer fit for many zone 9–10 gardens.

North Florida. UF/IFAS lists these as easy transplants: arugula, beets, broccoli, Brussels sprouts, cabbage, cauliflower, Chinese cabbage, collards, kale, kohlrabi, sugarcane, and Swiss chard. Transplant with care: celery, mustard, and spinach. Sow from seed: carrots, bulbing and bunching onions, radish, and turnips.

Central Florida. Easy transplants: arugula, beets, broccoli, Brussels sprouts, cabbage, cauliflower, Chinese cabbage, collards, endive, kale, kohlrabi, lettuce, sugarcane, and Swiss chard. Transplant with care: celery, mustard, pineapple, potatoes, and spinach. Sow from seed: carrots, bulbing and bunching onions, English peas, radish, and turnips.

Zones 11–13

The list below is UF/IFAS South Florida (south of State Road 70), a common fit for zone 10b–11 gardens in Florida. It does not cover Hawaii, Puerto Rico, or other zone 12–13 locations.

Easy transplants: arugula, beets, broccoli, Brussels sprouts, cabbage, cauliflower, Chinese cabbage, collards, eggplant, endive, kale, kohlrabi, lettuce, peppers, sugarcane, Swiss chard, tomatillo, tomatoes, and tropical spinaches. Transplant with care: amaranth, calabaza, celery, long squash, luffa, mustard, pineapple, potatoes, Seminole pumpkin, spinach, and sweet potatoes. Sow from seed: bush, lima, and pole beans; cantaloupes; carrots; corn; cucumbers; bunching onions; English and southern peas; radish; squashes; turnips; and watermelon.

Sources: University of Minnesota Extension, “Upper Midwest home garden care schedule” (reviewed in 2026); Penn State Extension, Dauphin County Master Gardener, Seasonal Garden Tips (written for USDA zone 6; no December page in that series); UF/IFAS Gardening Solutions, “Edibles to Plant in Florida”, December lists for North, Central, and South Florida.

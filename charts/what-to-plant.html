What to plant this week

Enter a US ZIP code or pick a USDA zone. The list uses your last spring and first fall 32°F dates and the same extension windows as the planting calendar.

Dates are averages. Local weather varies, and soil temperature is a better planting signal when you have it. A zone is a winter-low band, not a frost date. ZIP frost dates use the nearest NOAA station within 80 km. A zone with no ZIP uses the median of contiguous-US stations matched to that zone. Weeks run Sunday through Saturday.

Each plant name opens its page, with spacing and the source for the window. The full year is on the planting calendar.

Sources: 2023 USDA zone ZIP listings (PRISM Group, Oregon State University), NOAA 1991–2020 Climate Normals, and the extension windows in SOURCES.md. Census 2024 ZCTA points place each ZIP. Garlic clove dates follow University of Minnesota Extension, counted from the first 32°F date.

Lily (Lilium)

Clemson Extension calls lilies great cut flowers and says they provide color during most of the summer. The page does not list months, so this chart does not invent them.

South Carolina conditions. Summer- and Fall-Flowering Bulbs.

Dahlias, mums, tulips, and the other library flowers are on the bloom chart only when a page dates the bloom. They are left off this page because those pages do not call them cut flowers. No zone filter is on this page. The lily source does not give one.

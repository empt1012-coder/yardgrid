How it works

1

Add a bed

Pick the width and length in feet. Each square is one square foot.

2

Place plants

Drag a plant onto a square, or tap it and then tap the square. The square shows how many plants fit.

3

Use your frost date

Enter the average last spring frost. The calendar lists indoor, transplant, and direct-sow windows from extension guides.
